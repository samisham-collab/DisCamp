# DisCamp support

Email **{{ site.support_email }}**. We read every message, and we act on reports of objectionable content within 24
hours.

## Report a spot or a camper

On any spot's page in the app, scroll to **Something wrong?** and tap **Report this spot**, or **Report the name** for
the camper who added it. Reports are private: the camper isn't told who reported them. When three campers report the
same spot, it comes off the map until we review it.

## Block a camper

On a spot that camper added, tap **Block** at the bottom. You won't see spots they add. You can unblock them under
**Account > Blocked campers**. They aren't told.

## Delete your account

In the app, open **Account** and tap **Delete account** at the bottom. This removes your sign-in, your display name and
the campers you've blocked. Spots you added stay on the map without your name, so other campers can keep using them.
Delete any you'd rather remove first, from each spot's page.

If you signed in with Apple, also remove DisCamp from your Apple Account: on iPhone, open **Settings**, tap your name,
then **Sign in with Apple** (under **Sign-In & Security** on newer iPhones), choose DisCamp and tap **Delete**. On
other devices, sign in at [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com). Apple's help:
[Manage your apps with Sign in with Apple](https://support.apple.com/102571).

Can't open the app? Email {{ site.support_email }} from the address you signed in with, and we'll delete the account
for you.

## Spot information

Spots come from public listings and from campers. Roads, fees and rules change, so check with the land manager
before you go, and follow posted rules, fire restrictions and stay limits.
