Free dispersed camping map for iPhone and Android

Find a free campsite your rig can actually reach.

Set up your rig once: clearance, drivetrain, length and how you camp. Every pin on the map then tells you whether the road, the site and the price will work for you, before you drive out there.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Every pin answers one question: will it work for you?

DisCamp compares each spot's road and site with your rig, from a sedan with a tent to a 40-foot motorhome.

  • ✓Fits. The road and the site work for your rig.
  • !Tight. It should work, with a catch the spot spells out.
  • ×Won't fit. Too rough, too short, or no room to turn.
  • ?Not enough reports. Be the first to say.

Clearance, C1 to C7

Roads are rated from 2–4" (paved or smooth gravel) to 16" and up (obstacles that need a spotter), plus whether they need 4WD and wash out when wet.

How you camp

Tent, rooftop tent, van, trailer or big rig. Spots list who fits and the longest trailer campers have taken in.

What it costs

A cost meter from Free to $$$ a night. Trailers and big rigs see the RV fee, so a "free" spot that charges for hookups doesn't surprise you.

Built by the campers who use it

DisCamp starts with 59 spots across 24 states, from dispersed sites to KOAs, all from public sources. Campers add the rest:

  • Drop a pin, rate the road and the site, and add up to 3 photos.
  • Tap Still good when a spot is as described, or Something changed for a washout, a locked gate or a closure.
  • Two campers confirming a spot marks it confirmed. Every spot shows when it was last checked.

Leave it better: cleanups and Embers

Found trash at a site? Post a cleanup with a photo. Whoever packs it out sends an after photo and a shot of the haul, and a moderator checks it.

  • Approved cleanups earn Embers, and so do confirmed spots, check-ins and photos.
  • Ranks run from Tenderfoot to Guardian.
  • Embers are for bragging rights: no cash value, and they can't be bought.

Made to respect you

No account to browse

Search the map, read spots and set up your rig without signing in. You only need an account to post.

Location only when asked

DisCamp never asks for your location on launch. It stays on your phone unless you place a spot or a cleanup.

Works offline

The last spots you loaded stay on your phone for when the signal drops out.

No ads, no tracking

Nothing is sold or shared for advertising. Read the privacy policy.

Free, and paid for by tips

No ads, no paywalls, no premium tier. If DisCamp helps you find a spot, you can leave a tip in the app, in three sizes named for a coffee at camp, a bundle of firewood and a tank of gas. A tip doesn't unlock anything: it says thanks, and it pays for the server and the maps.
