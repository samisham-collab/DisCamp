# DisCamp

A free, community-built map of dispersed campsites for iPhone and Android. Set up your rig once, and every pin tells
you whether the road, the site and the price will work for you.

- [Support and contact](support/)
- [Privacy policy](privacy/)
- [Terms of use](terms/)

DisCamp is paid for by donations only: no ads, no paywalls, and no selling your data.
